## Prompts de geração

### Cabo

Create a production-ready isolated cutout image for an editorial documentary about submarine fiber optic internet cables. One thick real-world submarine fiber optic cable, diagonally curved in a gentle S from lower left to upper right, with the near end cut open showing layered black polymer jacket, fine steel armoring wires, a copper tube and tiny luminous cyan optical fibers inside. Accurate plausible engineered materials, refined photorealistic 3D product visualization, dark charcoal rubber, brushed metallic silver and copper, a few cyan luminous fiber tips, controlled studio rim lighting. The object must be COMPLETELY isolated on a REAL TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND (alpha), no floor, no horizon, no background color, no checkerboard baked in, no cast ground shadow. Leave generous transparent margins around the whole object. No words, no logos, no watermark, no labels. Cable only. Landscape 3:2 framing, finely detailed but clear silhouette, complete uncropped cable.

### Navio

Create a single isolated cutout asset for a sophisticated documentary motion graphic about submarine fiber optic cables. A realistic modern cable-laying research ship in a three-quarter elevated view, facing right, full vessel visible, elegant white upper superstructure, dark navy hull, safety orange cable reels and technical deck equipment, one thin black cable extending gently down from the stern. Photorealistic precision miniature/product render with realistic proportions, soft daylight from upper left and subtle cool blue edge light. COMPLETE REAL TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND with alpha. No sea, no water, no sky, no floor, no horizon, no ground shadow, no checkerboard background. Leave clear transparent margins around hull, antennas and cable. No readable text, no lettering, no brand, no watermark. Wide 3:2 composition.

### Satélite

Create a premium photorealistic 3D icon cutout of a single communications satellite for a documentary motion design. Entire satellite in three-quarter perspective, gold foil and silver central body, two symmetrical broad navy-blue solar panels, small white communications dish. Realistically engineered materials and restrained detail, visually legible as a compact icon, studio lighting with a cool edge rim. Satellite alone on genuine TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND with alpha. No planet, no space, no stars, no gradient, no ground, no shadows outside the object, no checkerboard baked in. Full object uncropped with generous transparent margin. No text, no watermark, no logo. Square composition.

## Limites de representação

As imagens são ilustrações geradas, não fotografias documentais de equipamentos identificados. A seção do cabo é ilustrativa; camadas e espessuras variam conforme o projeto. O gráfico de conexões não representa a localização de cabos reais.
